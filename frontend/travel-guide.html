When Should You Visit?

Nepal offers different experiences throughout the year.

Spring

A popular period for trekking with blooming rhododendrons and generally clear mountain views.

Summer

Monsoon conditions affect many regions, while some rain-shadow areas can remain suitable.

Autumn

A widely popular trekking period with favorable conditions and clear mountain scenery.

Winter

Lower elevations can be pleasant while high-altitude areas become colder and snowier.
